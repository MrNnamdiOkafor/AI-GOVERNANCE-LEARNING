# What is AI Governance?

## 1. Introduction
AI governance refers to the structures, policies, processes and controls used to ensure that artificial intelligence is developed and used responsibly.

## 2. Why AI Governance Matters
AI systems can create legal, ethical, operational and societal risks.

## 3. Key Areas
- Data protection
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Risk management
- Bias and discrimination
- Human oversight
- Security
- Regulatory compliance

  ## 4. My Initial Understanding
  AI governance is not simply about regulating AI.
  It is also about creating practical systems that allow organisations to identify, manage and monitor AI-related risks.
