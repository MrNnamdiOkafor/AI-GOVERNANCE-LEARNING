# AI Governance and Data Protection

## 1. Introduction

Artificial intelligence systems often depend on large amounts of data. This creates an important connection between AI governance and data protection law.

AI governance therefore cannot be considered only as a question of technology. It also involves legal, organisational, ethical and risk-management considerations.

## 2. Why Data Protection Matters

AI systems may collect, process, analyse or generate information relating to individuals.

Where personal data is involved, organisations need to consider issues such as:
- Lawfulness of processing
- Transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimisation
- Accuracy
- Security
- Accountability
- Individual rights

## 3. AI-Related data Protection Risks

Some potential risks include:

- Excessive collection of personal data
- use of personal data for incompatible purposes
- Lack of transparency about AI processing
- Inaccurate outputs
- Unauthorised access to data
- Risks associated with automated decision-making
- Difficulty explaining how an AI system reaches or supports a decision
 
## 4. AI Governance as a Practical Discipline

AI governance involves creating practical structures through which organisations can identify, assess, manage and monitor Ai-related risks.

This may include:

- Policies and procedures
- Risk assessments
- Human oversight
- Documentation
- Monitoring and auditing
- Accountability mechanisms
- Staff training
